---
check: Förkunskapskrav
---

## Purpose
<!-- sv 82f5bc0b -->

The *Förkunskapskrav* section (prerequisites; on du.se sometimes headed *Behörighet*, and the analysis treats the two words as the same) describes the earlier studies or equivalent qualifications needed for admission to the course. It matters both for admission decisions and for students planning their studies. A missing or incomplete section leads to uncertainty and uneven practice between subjects. The aim of the analysis is to find **where the section is missing altogether, where it exists only in English, and where the requirements refer to discontinued courses**: both courses that are probably discontinued (the name is not in the current catalogue) and courses confirmed as discontinued (du.se lists them as discontinued).

## Method
<!-- sv 6653365b -->

The Swedish section is read whether the page on du.se heads it *Förkunskapskrav* or *Behörighet*; the two are treated as the same section. Its English counterpart is *Prerequisites* (sometimes *Entry Requirements* or *Admission Requirements*) in the syllabus's English version. Four patterns are flagged:

1. **Section missing.** The syllabus has neither a Swedish nor an English version of the section.
2. **English version only.** `### Prerequisites` has content but `## Förkunskapskrav` is missing or empty. This is typically a sign of a gap in the Swedish page on du.se.
3. **Refers to a course probably discontinued.** The bullet names a specific course (the pattern *"kursen X, N hp"*, or a bullet that begins *"X, N hp"*), but no current course syllabus at Dalarna University, active or dormant, is called X. This probably means that the requirement is out of date and should be revised to the current course name. Only bullets that mention `hp` (credits) are examined. Upper secondary courses such as *Engelska 6* or *Matematik 2b* have no credits and are skipped by design.
4. **Refers to a course confirmed as discontinued.** The name matches a course that du.se has marked as discontinued. This is a sure signal: the course is still in Dalarna's historical catalogue but is no longer offered, so the requirement points to a course that students cannot take. If an active and a discontinued course share a name, the active one wins; the discontinued one is flagged only if no active course has the same name.

## Data source
<!-- sv bdb3b17d -->

- All course syllabi at Dalarna University, from du.se.
- The Swedish section *Förkunskapskrav* (also when du.se heads it *Behörighet*) and its English counterpart *Prerequisites*.
- The list of discontinued courses on du.se.

## Recommendations
<!-- sv 21ddc4df -->

1. **Fill in missing Swedish sections.** These come first where an English version already exists, since the content then only needs translating back.
2. **Update out-of-date course references.** When a requirement refers to a course that is no longer in Dalarna's catalogue, change it to the course's current name or to the course that replaced it. Check at the same time whether the requirements are still relevant to how the course is taught today. References to courses confirmed as discontinued (registered as discontinued on du.se, with course code and date) come before the probable ones: the former are certain, while the latter rest on the course name no longer being found in the catalogue.
3. **Raise the question in the quality committees concerned.** Should the schools share minimum requirements for how prerequisites are worded (for example always with credits and subject, or always with an example of an accepted qualification)?
