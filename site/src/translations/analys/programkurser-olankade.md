## Purpose
<!-- sv e83b9300 -->

The course list of a programme syllabus (the *Programmets kurser* section) should name each course exactly as its course syllabus does and link to it, so that the list can be trusted and followed. The analysis flags two kinds of difference: courses whose name in the programme text cannot be matched to any course syllabus, and courses where the programme text means the right course but writes its name differently from the official name in the syllabus. The aim is to tell real content errors in the programme syllabus apart from mere variants of a name, so that the right action can be taken.

In the table, the **Detail** column shows the course name as the programme syllabus writes it, and **Suggestion** the course we judge the row to mean (with its course code). A dash in Suggestion means that no candidate could be pointed out: either the course is not in Dalarna's catalogue, or the programme text is too ambiguous to point to a single course.

## Method
<!-- sv 94167519 -->

The course list of each programme syllabus (the *Programmets kurser* section) is gone through course by course. Rows that either have no link to their course syllabus or whose programme text differs from the syllabus's official name are classified:

| Category | Indicator |
|----------|-----------|
| **Unknown course reference in programme** | The name matches neither an active course syllabus nor the archive of discontinued courses |
| **Alternatives row (a choice between courses)** | The row offers a choice: it contains *eller* (or), *alternativt* (alternatively) or *valbar* (elective) |
| **Truncated course row** | An unclosed parenthesis, or a trailing *eller*/*och*/*,* at the end of the row |
| **Programme text differs from course name** | The course is linked, but the name in the programme text differs from the syllabus's official one (for example *System- och verksamhetsutveckling* against *System och verksamhetsutveckling*) |

Courses that match a **discontinued course syllabus** are reported separately in [[Nedlagda kursreferenser|Discontinued courses listed]] and are not covered here.

## Data source
<!-- sv c2a9ea72 -->

- All programme syllabi at Dalarna University.
- Active course names and course codes from the course syllabi.
- The list of discontinued courses on du.se.

## Recommendations
<!-- sv 7279e082 -->

1. **Unknown course reference in programme** is a content error in the programme syllabus: the course name does not match Dalarna's catalogue. Possible causes are a misspelling, a course never published on du.se, or a wrong name. Take it to the programme director.
2. **Alternatives row** is legitimate: the programme lets the student choose between courses. Nothing needs doing if the row is well worded.
3. **Truncated course row** is usually a broken-off sentence in the original programme text. Check the reported row against du.se.
4. **Programme text differs from course name** needs the programme director to update the course list in the programme syllabus, so that the text matches the syllabus's official name. The most common cause is that the programme text joins two names (*X- och Y* instead of *X och Y*).
5. **The Suggestion column is a starting point, not a decision.** Check the course code against du.se before correcting the programme text, especially when several courses have similar names (for example *… åk 7–9* against *… gymnasieskolan*).
6. **Rows without a suggestion are usually not name errors.** There are three recurring causes, all of which call for a conversation with the programme director rather than a correction of the text:
    - *The course syllabus has not been established yet.* New programmes get ahead of their courses. SPARG (established 2025-04-16) has all of year 1 linked and years 2–3 not linked at all.
    - *The course was never published as a syllabus of its own.* The theme courses in HAFSA and the courses in KMLJG are found neither among the active nor among the discontinued courses.
    - *The row is part of a course, not a course.* The sociology rows in SSHVG are content in the 30-credit courses [[GSO2XU]] and [[GSO2XV]], not syllabi of their own.
7. **Plain heading rows are not reported.** Rows such as *Inriktning sociologi II, 30 hp* name a block whose courses are listed as rows of their own beneath it, so they are left out.
