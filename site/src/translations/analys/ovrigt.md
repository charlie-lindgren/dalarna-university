## Purpose
<!-- sv dea94d52 -->

The `## Övrigt` section (other information) holds both course-specific information (joint teaching, older courses it replaces, restrictions in degrees) and a standard text on **targeted study support from Dalarna University**. The standard text matters for students with disabilities who need an adapted examination. When it is missing or worded differently in a syllabus, it becomes unclear which adaptations are possible. The aim of the analysis is to find **where the Övrigt section is missing altogether, and where the standard text on study support is not included**.

## Method
<!-- sv 45a34c77 -->

The `## Övrigt` section is extracted from the Swedish page. Two patterns are flagged:

1. **Section missing.** The syllabus has no `## Övrigt`.
2. **Standard text on study support missing.** The section exists but does not contain the anchor phrase *"pedagogiskt stöd från Högskolan Dalarna"*. The full standard text reads roughly: *"Om studenten har ett beslut/rekommendation om riktat pedagogiskt stöd från Högskolan Dalarna på grund av funktionsnedsättning, har examinator rätt att anpassa examinationen. Examinator avgör utifrån kursplanens mål om examinationen kan anpassas i enlighet med beslutet/rekommendationen."* (If the student has a decision or recommendation on targeted study support from Dalarna University because of a disability, the examiner may adapt the examination. The examiner decides, from the syllabus's outcomes, whether the examination can be adapted in line with the decision or recommendation.)

The analysis flags both syllabi where the text is missing entirely and syllabi with a different wording. Any legitimate variants (for example newer wordings adopted by a school) should then be harmonised jointly across all the schools.

## Data source
<!-- sv 8e4c15fa -->

- All course syllabi at Dalarna University, from du.se.
- Only the Swedish section `## Övrigt`.

## Recommendations
<!-- sv c4a5917d -->

1. **Add an Övrigt section** to syllabi that have none, with at least the standard text on study support.
2. **Harmonise the wording on study support.** Use the established text exactly, or adopt a shared revised wording across all four schools.
3. **Keep course-specific information** (joint teaching, replacements, contract education). The flag concerns only the standard text; the rest of the section is unaffected.
4. **Raise the question in the quality committees concerned.** Should Dalarna University set a central template for the Övrigt section, so that the standard text is always included when syllabi are written or revised?
