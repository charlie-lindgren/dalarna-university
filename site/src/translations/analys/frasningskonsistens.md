## Purpose
<!-- sv 8f2c925c -->

Learning outcomes usually open with a standard introductory phrase: *"Efter godkänd kurs ska studenten kunna:"* (on completion of the course, the student shall be able to). When the wording varies between syllabi, they become harder to read and for students to recognise. The aim is to find out **how many variants** are actually used at Dalarna University and how they are **distributed** across the four schools (IIT, IHV, IKS, ISLL).

## Method
<!-- sv 2a085f3f -->

In the `Lärandemål` section (learning outcomes, Swedish version), the first phrase is compared with the reference wording *"Efter godkänd kurs ska studenten kunna:"*. Differences are flagged. Typical variants concern the modal verb (*ska*/*skall*), the time phrase (*godkänd*/*avslutad*/*genomgången*), the subject (*studenten*/*den studerande*) or a missing colon.

Introductory text or part-course headings before the phrase are handled separately in [[Introfras|Introductory phrase]].

The only structural exception accepted is *"Efter avslutad delkurs ska den studerande kunna:"* (for a part of a course).

## Data source
<!-- sv 5091a95b -->

- All course syllabi at Dalarna University, from du.se.
- Only the Swedish section `Lärandemål`.

## Recommendations
<!-- sv 6d88b8eb -->

1. **Settle on one standard phrase.** *"Efter godkänd kurs ska studenten kunna:"* is the form the Bologna model recommends and should be the main form at Dalarna University.
2. **Mark courses for doctoral students** with *"Efter godkänd kurs ska doktoranden kunna:"* as an allowed variant.
3. **Raise the question in the quality committees concerned.** Should the introductory phrase be an explicit point in the schools' guidelines?
