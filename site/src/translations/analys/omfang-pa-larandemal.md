## Purpose
<!-- sv 1ddeae35 -->

Learning outcomes should be **few enough to be manageable** but **many enough to cover the course's content**. The number scales with the size of the course: a 7.5-credit course needs fewer outcomes than a 30-credit course.

| Credits | Min | Max |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ≤ 7.5 | 3 | 8 |
| 7.5–15 | 4 | 10 |
| 15–30 | 5 | 12 |
| > 30 | 6 | 15 |

Also, **no single outcome should be longer than 25 words**. If it is, it has become a sentence with several outcomes baked into it.

## Method
<!-- sv 6a3e2f26 -->

The bullets in the `Lärandemål` section (learning outcomes, Swedish version) are counted and compared with the table's range for the course's credits. Three kinds are flagged:

- **Too few outcomes.** Fewer than the minimum in the table above.
- **Too many outcomes.** More than the maximum in the table above.
- **Long outcome.** A single bullet of more than 25 words.

**Limitations:** Outcomes formatted as a numbered list (*1.*, *2.*, …) are not counted, which gives false negatives. Long bullets sometimes combine things legitimately (*"x, y, samt z"*) without that being a sign of poor division.

## Data source
<!-- sv 54bed1b7 -->

- All course syllabi at Dalarna University, from du.se (IIT, IHV, IKS, ISLL).
- Only the Swedish section `Lärandemål`.

## Recommendations
<!-- sv 35f7ac3e -->

1. **Review each flagged syllabus by hand.** The guideline is advisory, not absolute.
2. **Split long bullets** at the next revision. If an outcome holds several outcomes, make it several bullets.
3. **A numbered list instead?** If a school wants to move to numbered lists, that should be a deliberate decision, written into the templates.
