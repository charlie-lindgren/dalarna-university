## Innehåll

Kursen börjar med individorienterade analyser av organisatoriskt beteende. Begreppen jobbattityder, känslor och personlighetsfaktorer, perception samt individuellt beslutsfattande presenteras och diskuteras. Särskild uppmärksamhet ägnas åt motivationsteorier.
 

Vidare fokuserar kursen på ledarskap och grupp/teambeteenden. Förutsättningarna för ledarskap och skillnaderna mellan ledarskap och management diskuteras. Olika ledarskapskoncept och teorier förklaras, liksom trender och utvecklingen inom området för att illustrera hur synen på ledarskap har förändrats över tid. En viktig del av kursen behandlar sociala och etiska aspekter av ledarskap och potentiellt missbruk av makt.
 

Vidare behandlas hur man leder team effektivt, vilka faktorer som kan hindra grupp- och/eller teamprestanda och hur man kan leda en organisatorisk förändring.

## English Version

### Learning Outcomes

_Knowledge and Understanding_  
Upon completion of the course, students shall be able to:

  - explain basic concepts, models and theoretical foundations in the field of organisational behaviour and individual motivation, and use them to resolve specific organisational issues at different levels (individual, group as well as organisation) (1)

  - explain basic concepts, models and theoretical foundations in the field of leadership, and distinguish between leadership and management (2)

_Competence and Skills_  
After completing the course, students shall be able to:

  - identify and analyse the principal factors that hinder individual, group and team effectiveness and engagement in different organisational settings (3)

  - critically analyse leadership roles and discuss the impact of leadership in terms of the individual and the organisation/society (4)

_Judgement and Approach_  
Upon completion of the course, students shall be able to:

  - demonstrate the ability to make judgements with regard to social and ethical aspects in the field of leadership (5)

### Course Content

The course starts with focus on the individual level of analysis of organisational behaviour. The concepts job attitudes, emotions and personality factors, perception and individual decision making are presented and discussed. Special attention is given to motivation theories.

Further the course focuses on leadership and group/team behaviour. The prerequisites for leadership and the differences between leadership and management are discussed. Different leadership concepts and theories are explained, as are trends and development in the field to illustrate how the view of leadership has changed over time. An important part of the course deals with social and ethical aspects of leadership and the potential abuse of power.  
  
Students also learn how to lead teams effectively, what factors might hinder group and/or team performance, and how to head organisational change.

Examination 3 (learning outcomes 4 and 5): Group presentation in the seminar and individual written reflection, 1.5 credits.
